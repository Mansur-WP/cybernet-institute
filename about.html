About Cybernet

Dedicated to Practical Computer Education

Empowering individuals and learners in Kano with real-world digital skills since 2020.

Our Mission & Training Focus

At Cybernet Computer Institute, our goal is to bridge the digital skills gap by providing structured, accessible, and highly practical computer training.

We believe true digital competence comes from direct practice. Our training center in Kano provides students, job seekers, and working professionals with the hands-on experience needed to type, create professional documents, analyze data with spreadsheets, present ideas, and responsibly leverage modern AI tools.
